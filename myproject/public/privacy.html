Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what we never do with it.

Effective date: August 1, 2026

1. The short version

We collect the minimum needed to run Lisa 1000: your account email, the stories you create, and anonymous usage counts. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe — we never see your card number. We don't sell your data, we don't run third-party ad trackers, and the service is not directed at children.

2. What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and sign-in credentials when you create an account.
  • Your content — the story prompts you enter and the stories, illustrations, and audio generated for you, so they appear in your library.
  • Usage information — how many stories you've generated in the current period (used to apply the free-tier limit, credits, and the usage-aware renewal reminders described on the pricing page).
  • Billing information — handled by Stripe. We store only what's needed to link your account to your subscription or credits (for example a Stripe customer ID and subscription status) — never card numbers.

3. How we use it

To provide the service: generating and saving your stories, enforcing plan limits, processing purchases, and sending renewal reminders before every subscription charge. If reminder emails are enabled, they're sent through our email provider solely for that purpose. We don't use your content to advertise to you, and we don't sell or rent personal information to anyone.

4. Children's privacy

Lisa 1000 is made for parents, guardians, and educators — not for children. Accounts may only be created by adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The details you include in a story prompt (like a child's first name) are stored only as part of your story, under your account and your control. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

5. Where your data lives

Data is stored with Cloudflare (our hosting, database, and media storage provider) and processed by Stripe for payments. These providers act as processors on our behalf and have their own security and privacy commitments.

6. Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and your preferences (like translation language) working. We don't use third-party advertising or analytics cookies.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access or export — your stories are visible and downloadable from your library at any time.
  • Delete — you can delete individual stories in the app, or email us to delete your entire account and its data.
  • Billing — manage or cancel your subscription any time from the billing portal on the pricing page.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information — email us and we'll honor them.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll post a notice on the site before they take effect. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version.
