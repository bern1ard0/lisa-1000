Accounts are for adults — parents, guardians, and educators. You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live) to create an account or make a purchase. Children may enjoy the stories together with you, but the account and everything in it is yours and your responsibility.

3. Plans, credits, and billing

  • Free plan — signed-in users can generate up to 5 stories per calendar month. Browsing and reading the public library is free for everyone.
  • Premium — a monthly subscription with unlimited story generation. It renews automatically each month until you cancel.
  • Credit packs — a one-time purchase of story credits (1 credit = 1 story). Credits never expire and are used automatically once your free monthly stories run out.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Prices are shown on the pricing page before you buy.

4. Renewals, reminders, and cancelling

We believe you should never be surprised by a charge. Before every Premium renewal we show you a reminder in the app (and by email if enabled). If you've barely used Premium in a billing period, the reminder will say so and suggest cancelling or switching to credits instead.

You can cancel any time from the billing portal (Manage billing on the pricing page). Cancelling stops future charges; you keep Premium until the end of the period you've already paid for, then you move back to the Free plan. Your saved stories always remain yours.

5. Refunds

Monthly subscription payments for a period that has already started are generally not refunded — cancelling instead prevents the next charge. If you believe you were charged in error, or you bought a credit pack and haven't used any of the credits, contact us within 14 days and we'll make it right.
